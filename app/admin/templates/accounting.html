Not classified is tax that was charged and collected on an order with no recorded place of supply. It is real tax that cannot be filed under CGST/SGST or IGST, so it has a column of its own rather than being dropped - every row's taxable value plus its four tax figures equals its total, which is what makes these sums reconcile to the paisa.
